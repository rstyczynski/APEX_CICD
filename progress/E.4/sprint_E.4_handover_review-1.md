## R1-L1 — distinguish worker overlap from native-process overlap

**Location and observed evidence:** Presentation slide 13 says, “All three native process intervals overlapped by about 25 seconds.” Similar wording occurs in the implementation's fixed-fixture/capacity discussion and the test report's IT-1 explanation. [parallel-contributions.json](tests/parallel-contributions.json) gives approximately 24.84 seconds of common overlap for the broader per-author intervals. However, the individual `author-a/b/c-capture.log.process.json` receipts give a common SQLcl capture interval from 12:35:34.549596 to 12:35:49.306891 UTC: **14.757295 seconds**. The three `author-a/b/c-stage-verify.log.process.json` receipts give a second common native interval from 12:35:52.329607 to 12:35:56.484836 UTC: **4.155229 seconds**. The worker intervals include time outside those native subprocesses.

**Impact and inference:** The current sentence overstates the measured duration of native concurrency and obscures which clock interval is being reported. This is a measurement-label issue, not evidence that the work was serial. Distinct native PIDs and overlapping intervals establish the required process concurrency, and the single-operator role simulation is disclosed consistently.

**Correction:** Describe approximately 25 seconds of overlapping author-worker activity, including approximately 14.76 seconds of three-way native capture overlap and approximately 4.16 seconds of three-way native stage/verify overlap. Alternatively, omit the duration and state that both native phases overlapped. Reconcile slide 13 and current narrative summaries without rewriting historical receipts.

**Acceptance check:** Recompute intersection duration as minimum finish minus maximum start separately for the three capture receipts and the three stage/verify receipts. Confirm the revised presentation and prose identify the corresponding measurement accurately. No fresh Oracle execution is necessary.
